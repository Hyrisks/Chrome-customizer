FEATURES

Everything you wish Google’s page had.

Pick what you like. It all saves automatically and works on both your new tab and google.com.

🖼️

Any background

A colour, an image, a GIF or an MP4/WebM video. Paste a link or upload a file up to 200 MB.

🎞️

Slideshow

Mix pictures, GIFs and videos, set the order, and it loops back to the first one with a smooth fade.

✋

Drag to frame it

If a picture doesn’t fit, drag it to choose what part shows, and scroll to zoom.

🔗

Quick links box

Right-click to add as many site icons as you like. Move it, resize it, make it yours.

Logo or clock

Keep the Google logo, hide it, fade it, or swap it for the time, solid or translucent.

The new tab’s search bar is see-through glass, so your background shows right through it.

Cleaner Google

100% Open Source

Private by design

HOW IT WORKS

One engine, two pages.

The same code draws your background on the new tab and on google.com, so they always match.

1️⃣

You customize

Click the small 🖼️ button in the corner to open the panel. Changes apply instantly.

2️⃣

It’s saved locally

Settings live in Chrome’s extension storage and big files in the extension’s own database.

3️⃣

Every tab updates

Open tabs update live, so change it once and it shows everywhere.

FAQ

Good to know.

Why is there a bar under my new tab?

Chrome adds it to extension-made new tab pages. Right-click it and choose “Hide footer on New Tab page”. Extensions can’t remove it.

My link won’t load as a background.

Use a direct link to the file (ending in .gif, .png, .jpg, .mp4 or .webm), or download it and use Upload instead.

Which sites does it run on?

Only your new tab page and google.com / google.ca. You can add other Google domains in the manifest.

Does it collect my data?

No. Nothing leaves your browser. Site icons are fetched from Google’s favicon service, and a background link loads from the site that hosts it.
